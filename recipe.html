Alfredo Pasta

Ingredients:

  • Chicken fillet 250g
  • Cooking oil 2tbs
  • Salt 1/2tsp
  • Black pepper powder 1/2tsp
  • Garlic powder 1/2tsp
  • Italian seasoning 1/2tsp
  • Soy sauce 1tbs
  • Butter 2-3tbs
  • Cream cheese spread 150g
  • Cream 1/2 cup
  • Chicken stock 1 cup
  • Black pepper crushed 1/4tsp
  • Nutmeg 1 pinch
  • Fettuccine pasta
  • Fresh parsley chopped 2-3tbs

    • Directions:

    1. Cut the chicken into long pieces and set aside.
    2. Pour cooking oil in a pot and add chicken in it,mix well until it changes color.
    3. Add salt,black pepper powder,garlic powder,italian seasoning & soy sauce,cook until it dries up (Approx.4-5mins),then set aside.
    4. Put butter in a pot,let it melt.
    5. Then add cream cheese spread on low flame & mix them well.
    6. Then add cream & again mix them well.
    7. Add chicken stock,whisk continuously.
    8. Add black pepper (crushed),salt,italian seasoning,nutmeg & garlic powder,whisk well.
    9. Cook on low flame for 2-3mins.
    10. Now add cooked chicken & mix well.
    11. Add boiled fettuccine pasta,give it a good mix.
    12. In the end add fresh parsley for garnishing.
    13. Serves 2-3 people.
